## Terms

The terms used in the Conformance Level information are defined as follows:

- **Supports**: the functionality of the product has at least one method that meets the criterion without known defects, or meets it with equivalent facilitation. Here it also means that a test shows it.
- **Partially Supports**: some functionality of the product does not meet the criterion. The remarks say which.
- **Does Not Support**: the majority of product functionality does not meet the criterion.
- **Not Applicable**: the criterion is not relevant to the product. The remarks say why.
- **Not Evaluated**: the product has not been evaluated against the criterion. Used for WCAG level AAA, and for the functional performance statements, which this report does not assess on their own.
